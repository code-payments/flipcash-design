# Design decisions

Calls that generalise, with their why, so nobody re-litigates them. Newest first. A decision that
becomes a rule also lands in `design-standard.md`. Open items carry ◇.

Most decisions were made on the iOS app. "iOS:" marks the file that is the evidence for a call; the files each decision touched on iOS are listed in the iOS repo's `.claude/docs/design/implementation.md`. Plan and audit references below (`.claude/plans/…`, audits A–D) are in or from the iOS repo.
